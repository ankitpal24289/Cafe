Our story

The Global Table is a neighbourhood café in Airoli where every plate carries a little of somewhere else. We cook with care, plate with creativity, and keep the room spotless and warm — so a quick coffee turns into the long catch-up you didn’t plan. Come hungry, stay for the people.
